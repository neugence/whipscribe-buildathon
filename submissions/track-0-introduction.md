## What running my shipped apps taught me

Building and deploying applications taught me that making a feature work is
only one part of building a product. Users also need clear navigation,
understandable information, and predictable loading, success, and failure
states.

While working on the Centralized Training & Placement Portal, I had to account
for different student and administrator workflows, including eligibility,
application status, job-posting lifecycles, and administrative actions. That
made me more conscious of the difference between implementing a feature and
making the overall experience understandable and useful.
