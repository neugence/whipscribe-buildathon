Quarterly planning review

1m 44s · English

Thanks, everyone, for joining the quarterly planning review. I want to keep this quick, because a few of you have a hard stop at the top of the hour.

First, the numbers. Revenue came in eleven percent above forecast, and most of that came from the enterprise segment, where three of the five deals we expected next quarter closed early.

Self-serve was flat. Trials were up, but conversion to paid slipped by about a point and a half, and we think that is mostly the onboarding change we shipped in July.

Second, the mobile launch. We are moving it from October to the first week of November. The accessibility audit found eleven issues, four of them blocking, and I would rather ship late than ship something half our users can't navigate.

Third, hiring. We have two engineering offers out and one accepted. Support is still the bottleneck; response times went from four hours to nine over the summer.
