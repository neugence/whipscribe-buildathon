The transcript reader on a phone, the next pass

A phone reader is for reading. This pass gives the words the screen, asks for the sign-up when there is a reason to, and stops showing controls that cannot work. It removes more than it adds.

  • The words get the screen. Nothing is pinned while you read a preview. The header comes back when you scroll up.
  • Ask at the moment of intent. Where the preview ends, when you pick a download, when you search past the preview. Not on every screen.
  • Only what can work. No play button without audio, no rename on someone else's file, no tabs on an error.
  • Say it in words. "1 min 5 s left", not "48%". "A problem on our side", not "HTTP 503".

The questions in the brief

What did a person on a phone come here to do, and how many taps away is it?
Read what was said, or find one thing that was said. Reading is zero taps and now gets the whole screen. Finding is one tap: the search field sits at the top of the transcript, where phones put it, instead of behind an icon.
The keep-reading bar and the player take the bottom. Right trade? And with the keyboard up?
No. A permanent bar is ignored after a few seconds and costs 157 px on every screen. The ask now appears where people decide: at the end of the preview, when they pick a download, and when search runs out of preview. The player only appears when there is audio. With the keyboard up, search is its own layer and nothing else is pinned to the bottom.
The More menu has nine items. Which belong?
Four for the owner (text size and display, rename, file details, move to trash) and two for a guest. Home duplicated the back arrow. Timestamps duplicated reading settings. Sign in belongs to the preview asks. Copy went to Download. Quiz went to AI Chat.
Search, download, settings: three ways to open a panel. Should they feel like one?
Download, More, text size, sign-up, rename and details are now one sheet component: same handle, title, close button and row style. Search stays different on purpose. It needs the keyboard and the full screen, so it is a mode rather than a panel.
What does the reader look like while the transcript is processing?
Designed from the two states the API reports (queued, processing with a progress value), plus the outcomes it documents: done, failed (no minutes used) and no speech found (speech_detected: false, not charged). See screens 13 and 14.
Four speakers at 320 px?
Each turn starts with a coloured initial and the speaker's name, and consecutive lines from one person merge into a paragraph. Tapping the name renames that speaker everywhere, with the first thing they said as a reminder. See screen 15.
After you select a line on a phone, what should happen?
Tap a line and its actions appear at the bottom, in thumb reach: play from there (only with audio), copy with the time and file name, share, ask AI about it. Long-press still gives the phone's own text selection. The prototype no longer draws a second menu over it.
The desktop page is unchanged. Should it be?
Mostly yes, since the layout works on a wide screen. But the shared pieces should carry over: paragraphs instead of one row per segment, the preview ask at the end rather than above the first line, and the copy. Screen 18 lists what I would change.
Built as a static prototype: HTML, CSS and one script, no build step, made-up recordings in the shapes the API documents. Notes, method and what is not done: README.md.
