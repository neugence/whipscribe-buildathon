The transcript reader on a phone, next pass

Every "current" screen below is WhipScribe's own screenshot from challenges/01-mobile-transcript/after/. Every "next" screen is live HTML at the same width, so it can be opened on a phone. The notes under each pair say what I measured on the current screen, what I changed, and what I kept.

Measured

  • Header and tabs take 118pt. The preview bar and player take 158pt. That leaves 568 of 844pt (67%) for text.
  • The player shows a large green Play button and "00:00 / 00:00" while a third row says "Audio unavailable". It takes 102pt and cannot play anything.
  • The preview message is cut off: "Create a free account to read...". The part that sells (first transcript free, private, never used to train AI) only exists on desktop.
  • Text is justified with hyphenation ("seg-ment"). Desktop is left-aligned.
  • Lines break mid-sentence: 0:03 ends "have a hard", 0:07 starts "stop at the top of the hour."
  • The meta line ends in a stray separator: "1m 44s · EN ·".

Changed

  • One bottom bar instead of two. When audio is unavailable there is no player at all, only the preview line (64pt). With a one-row title (56pt) and the tab row (46pt), the text area is 678pt (80%).
  • Left-aligned text, no hyphenation.
  • A line never ends mid-sentence: consecutive segments are joined until the sentence ends, and the first segment's time is shown. Tapping still plays from that second.
  • Download moves up next to More; search stays next to the tabs.

Kept: the three tabs, the timestamp gutter, the "Keep reading" wording and colour.

The questions in the challenge

Answers

What did a person on a phone come to do, and how many taps away is it?

Read, and for a preview visitor, keep the transcript. Reading was zero taps but on half the screen at 320; now it is most of the screen. Keeping it was two taps from the end of the preview (Keep reading, then Continue with Google in a modal); now it is one, on a card right where the preview ends.

The Keep reading bar and the player take the bottom. Right trade? And with the keyboard up?

Not both at once. There is one bottom bar: the preview line when there is no audio, a player when there is. It hides while the keyboard is up and while the sign-up card is on screen.

The More menu has nine items. Which belong there?

Five for the owner: Copy transcript, Quiz me, Rename, File details, Delete. Home goes (the back arrow does it), Timestamps goes (it is in reading settings), Reading settings stops being a second sheet and sits at the top of the same one. Sign in replaces the owner actions for visitors.

Search, download, settings: should they feel like one?

Download and settings, yes: both are the same bottom sheet. Search, no: you type and read at the same time, so it stays inline in the top bar and never covers the text.

What does the reader look like while processing?

Screen 10: the job's steps, a skeleton where text will appear, and a clear "you can close this page". Summary and Chat tabs appear only when they have something to show.

Four speakers at 320?

Screen 9: a name and colour dot above each turn, lines grouped under it, times in the gutter.

What should happen after selecting a line?

Screen 8: Copy, Copy with time link, Ask AI about this, and Play only when there is audio, in the bottom bar.

The desktop page is unchanged. Should it be?

Mostly yes. Two transcript-level fixes carry over: joined sentences and no dead player.

Kept on purpose

What I did not change

  • The three tabs and their order. They are clear and short.
  • Inline search with a match count and up/down. It works; it only needed to stop stacking.
  • Bottom sheets for download and settings.
  • The timestamp gutter, tap a line to play, and "Keep reading" as the words on the button.
  • Google first on sign-up.
