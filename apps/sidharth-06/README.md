## What running your shipped apps taught you

No store releases yet, so the honest version: shipping Swaram and Enlight
taught me that the last 10% — states, errors, empty screens, docs a stranger
can follow — is the difference between a demo and a product. That is exactly
what I focused on in the Challenge 01 entry: the reader now has designed
failure, deleted, wrong-account, empty and offline states, not just a happy
path.
