## Things I've built

I pulled the commit counts from GitHub and opened every link on 24 Sep 2026.

**Export Management System** · [ems.dhandco.in](https://ems.dhandco.in)
Built for a chartered accountant whose clients are exporters. It tracks export
orders, forward contracts, payments and BRCs, and flags the mistakes that cost
them money: an order with no forward contract booked, or a contract left
hanging after the order changed. The first version ran on Google Sheets and
Apps Script. I'm now moving it to Next.js and Supabase with proper logins per
client. Private repo because it's client work. 141 of its 145 commits are mine
and I can give reviewers read access.

**CricOracle** · [live](https://cric-oracle.vercel.app) · [code](https://github.com/ayan-joshi/cric-oracle)
Ask any question about the laws of cricket and get the answer with the exact
MCC law it comes from. RAG over the 42 Laws and the ICC playing conditions,
using pgvector on Supabase. All 14 commits are mine.

**InvoiceKit** · [live](https://invoicekit-sigma.vercel.app) · [code](https://github.com/ayan-joshi/Invoicekit)
Indian Shopify sellers upload their orders CSV and get GST invoices back as
PDFs, with CGST, SGST or IGST picked automatically. Next.js front end, FastAPI
and ReportLab behind it. All 8 commits are mine.

**Baby Choice Guide** · [babychoiceguide.com](https://babychoiceguide.com)
A baby product guide and awards site on Next.js and Supabase. Private repo,
all 94 commits mine.

**ProductPulse** · [code](https://github.com/ayan-joshi/Productpulse)
Give it a product idea and it goes through Reddit and Hacker News for real
complaints, then streams back a report in about a minute and a half. All 5
commits are mine. It's on Render's free plan, so the live link can take a
minute to wake up.

**PolicyClarity** · [live](https://frost-three.vercel.app) · [code](https://github.com/ayan-joshi/Policy-Clarity)
Turns long legal policies into plain summaries. Built with two other people;
42 of the 58 commits are mine.

**Nubokind Smart Inventory** · [code](https://github.com/IsHa0102/Nubokind-smart-inventory)
A teammate's inventory tool for Nubokind. I moved it off its Express server
onto our shared Supabase database and added purchase order tracking. Merged:
[#10](https://github.com/IsHa0102/Nubokind-smart-inventory/pull/10),
