**What running your shipped apps taught me**

Building and maintaining real products taught me that shipping is very different from simply completing a coding task.

I've learned to think about the complete user journey: what the user sees first, how they understand a feature, what happens when something goes wrong, and what happens after the feature is actually released.

I've also learned to balance product requirements with technical constraints, debug issues that only appear in real environments, and iterate based on actual usage rather than assuming the first implementation is good enough.

Working across both professional products and independent projects has also taught me the importance of ownership — understanding the problem, making decisions, implementing the solution and taking responsibility for the result.
